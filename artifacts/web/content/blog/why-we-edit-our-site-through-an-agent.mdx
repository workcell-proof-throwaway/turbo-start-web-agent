---
title: Why we edit our site through an agent
description: Why we make site changes by asking an agent in plain words, and what that changes for the people who write the copy.
pubDate: 2026-10-04
category: Guides
excerpt: Asking an agent for a change is faster than filing a ticket, and every edit is still reviewed before it goes live.
---

Most site changes are small: a new headline, a corrected price, a fresh question for the FAQ. Waiting on a developer for each one slows everyone down. With an agent, the person who knows what the page should say describes the change in plain words and gets the edit back to look at.

Speed is not the only reason. The agent can only change content, and every change is checked and reviewed before visitors see it. The people who write the words stay in control of them, and the developers keep a clear record of exactly what changed.
