---
title: Why we edit our site through an agent
description: How a chat replaced our content tickets
pubDate: 2026-10-05
category: Guides
excerpt: Asking an agent for small site changes keeps them quick and keeps the people who know the content in charge of it.
---

Most changes to a marketing site are small. A headline needs a rewrite, a price changes, a new question
turns up on the FAQ. Asking an agent for those edits means the person who knows the content can make the
change themselves, in plain words, without filing a ticket and waiting in a developer's queue.

The agent only works with content and the sections already built for the site, and every change is
reviewed before it goes live. Developers keep the same history and review they always had, so the site
stays safe to change. The people closest to the words get to decide what the words say.
