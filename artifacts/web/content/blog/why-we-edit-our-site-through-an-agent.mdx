---
title: Why we edit our site through an agent
description: Why our team asks an agent to change the site instead of opening an admin panel or waiting on a developer.
pubDate: 2026-10-03
category: Guides
excerpt: Editing the site is now a conversation. Here is why we made that choice and what it changed for the team.
---

We used to treat a small change to the site as a small project. Someone had to find the right page, learn the right admin screen, or ask a developer to fit it in. Now we describe the change in plain words to an agent, and it makes the edit for us.

The agent can only change content, and every edit is checked before it can reach visitors and reviewed by a person first. That keeps the speed of a conversation without giving up the care of a normal review, so anyone on the team can suggest a change and trust that nothing breaks along the way.
